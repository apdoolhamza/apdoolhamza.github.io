Attractions overview

Attraction Best Time Entry Fee Notes
Kofar Mata Dye Pits Morning · 9–11am ₦500 Watch cloth being dyed and hung out to dry
Afternoon · 2–4pm ₦500 Quieter visit; good light for photos of the finished cloth
Kurmi Market Morning · 8am–1pm Free entry Busiest and best-stocked before midday; bring cash for bargaining
Gidan Makama Museum Anytime · 9am–5pm ₦300 A 15th-century palace tracing Kano's history - allow an hour
The Durbar Festival runs during Eid celebrations - check the Islamic calendar each year for exact dates

Fees are approximate and may change - confirm locally before you travel.
